Method notes

Hierarchy. Supplier-specific: kg × supplier PCF (DQ 1 if third-party verified, else 2). Average-data: kg × industry factor (DQ 3). Spend-based: USD × EEIO factor (DQ 4). Uncertainty uses ±10/20/30/50% by DQ, combined as root-sum-square.

Levers. Renewable power removes the electricity-related share of a category's footprint that is not already renewable, ramping linearly to the target year for engaged suppliers. Material switch moves 50% of aluminium to recycled and 50% of steel to EAF. Suppliers with a science-based target cut 4.2% a year; engaged suppliers adopt one from 2027.

1.5 °C line. Linear absolute contraction of 4.2% a year from the base year. Emission factors are illustrative placeholders; swap in USEEIO, EXIOBASE or ecoinvent values before using the numbers.
